# Gachiakuta

**Gachiakuta** is my favorite manga and series.  
It’s full of **action**, **mystery**, and **beautiful art**.

---

##  Overview
- **Genre:** Action, Fantasy, Drama  
- **Author:** Kei Urana  
- **Illustrator:** Hideyoshi Andou  
- **Status:** Ongoing  

---

##  Main Characters
1. **Rudo** – The main protagonist who was cast into the abyss.
2. **Enjin** – A mysterious figure with great power.
3. **Zanka** – Rudo’s mentor and guide.

---

##  Why I Like It
> “Gachiakuta’s art style is unique, the story is intense, and the world-building is amazing.”

---

##  Fun Fact
The creator of *Gachiakuta* was once an assistant of Atsushi Ohkubo, the creator of *Fire Force* and *Soul Eater*!

---

###  Recommended For:
If you like:
- Gritty, emotional storytelling  
- Amazing artwork  
- Strong character development  

Then you’ll definitely love **Gachiakuta**!
